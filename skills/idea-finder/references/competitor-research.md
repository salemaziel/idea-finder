# Competitor and alternative research

Start with the candidate uncertainty: whether this segment already solves the problem adequately, what it spends, or why an alternative fails. Include doing nothing, manual/DIY processes, services, incumbent and adjacent tools; investigate funded startups only when relevant. Capture source/date, target segment, workflow coverage, pricing evidence, reported strengths/gaps and source limits. Negative reviews are a selected sample, not prevalence or proof of whitespace.

Use primary product/pricing material and specific customer accounts when available. Label missing pricing or inferred gaps unknown. Ask why satisfied customers choose their workaround; absence of a tool does not prove an opportunity. Use [incumbent prompts](incumbent-risk.md) where relevant.

Estimate market size only if it could change this founder's decision. Show buyer definition, dated source/range, price assumptions and sensitivity; never invent counts or multiply a broad population by an unsupported price. Small-business income feasibility and venture expansion potential are separate questions, with no universal TAM cutoff. Store findings in COMPETITOR RESEARCH and relevant ROLE RESEARCH; update candidate assumptions and the next test.
