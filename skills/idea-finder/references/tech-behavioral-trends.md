# Technology and behavioral shifts

Treat proposed trends as hypotheses, not evidence of customer demand. Investigate a shift only if it affects the current problem or decision. Verify dated primary sources for capability, adoption, cost and limitations in the actual setting. Do not assume a demographic group's behavior, tool budget or tolerance of manual work.

Record a supported shift, source/date, relevant workflow, implication and remaining uncertainty in TECH BREAKTHROUGHS or ROLE RESEARCH. Label inference separately. “No demonstrated relevant change” is valid; do not manufacture a why-now narrative. Keep discovery centered on the problem until the founder chooses solution experiments.
