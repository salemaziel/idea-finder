# Contextual risk prompts

These prompt investigation, not automatic elimination. Relate each relevant risk to the founder's goal, resources, actual buyer and workflow. Record supporting and contrary evidence and the test that could change the assessment.

| Risk | Question to investigate |
| --- | --- |
| Generic AI assistant or dashboard | Which specific incident has consequences, and is a standalone problem established? |
| Platform dependence | What access/control is required, and what happens if terms or features change? |
| Consumer network or marketplace | Can a narrow initial group get value without broad adoption? |
| Regulation or liability | What expertise, permissions and obligations apply to this workflow and founder? |
| Price competition | Why would this customer change, and can delivery remain economic? |
| Behavior change | What change is actually required, and what has the customer already tried? |
| Enterprise purchase cycle | Who pays, how long does approval take, and does the founder have runway and access? |
| Incumbent overlap | Is the exact segment well served, and what contrary evidence exists? |
| Rare occurrence | How costly is an event, and is prevention or risk reduction already funded? |

Recommend pause only with explicit reasoning about evidence and constraints, not category membership or a score threshold. Revisit when evidence changes.
