# Focused command guidance

Use the existing record and ask only the next relevant two or three questions; a requested deliverable need not end in questions. Read the canonical [assessment](opportunity-ranking.md) when assessing. Record new facts and unresolved questions under existing headings, with sources and clear labels.

## dig

Choose one recent incident in the named role. Ask what happened immediately before and after, who handled it, and the consequence. Follow answers into the exact workaround, time/cost, failed alternatives, who bears the loss and who can authorize spending. Explore a surprising connection to another life/work role only when the founder's account supports it. Reflect the tentative interpretation back before promoting it to a finding. If no painful incident emerges, consider that the candidate may not matter rather than manufacturing one. Update ROLE RESEARCH, relevant CANDIDATE PROBLEMS and OPEN QUESTIONS with quotes, contradictions and the next uncertainty.

## persona

Build a bounded profile from evidence: role/context, relevant workflow/day, triggering incident, consequences, tools/workarounds, attempted alternatives, user versus payer, access route. Cite the supporting record items. Mark missing details unknown and inferred connective narrative explicitly; do not invent names, demographics, quotes, budgets or a typical day. Explain whose reports the profile covers and where they disagree. Store it in the relevant ROLE RESEARCH entry and link candidate assumptions. A persona is a synthesis, never new demand evidence.

## sim

Open with “Practice only: this customer and their statements are fictional.” Ground the setup in known context while labeling invented objections. Play a skeptical customer one exchange at a time, resisting leading questions and pitches; do not coach the founder to convince the customer the pain is real. After practice, debrief question neutrality, incident specificity, assumptions and one question to try with a real person. Do not award demand, WTP or candidate-score credit for fictional responses. Save only a labeled practice debrief in INTERVIEW SCRIPTS, never INTERVIEW DATA; put untested questions in OPEN QUESTIONS.

## compare

Compare A and B side by side: five dimensions (unknowns intact), evidence confidence, access, contradictions, effort/resources, fit to the founder's goal and crucial unresolved assumptions. State the actual tradeoff: what favors each and what evidence could reverse the choice. Keep venture considerations separate. Recommend an investigation or conditional preference only when supported; “none is ready” and “pause this candidate” are valid. Do not break ties by fabricating a total. Update candidate reasoning and the next test in VALIDATION PLAN.

## why-me

Assess domain knowledge, network access, credibility, ability/resources to address the workflow and sustained interest. Name demonstrated strengths, unknowns and the most consequential gap. Avoid a separate competing numerical score; use the canonical founder-fit dimension when a score helps. Recommend a feasible way to test or reduce the gap, preserving the founder's stated constraints. Update relevant candidate evidence and OPEN QUESTIONS.

## next

Identify the unresolved uncertainty most likely to change the decision, not the next step of a fixed funnel. Give one action sized to the founder's time and access:
- uncertainty and why it matters;
- action, target person/source, time box and cost if any;
- expected learning;
- success signal and disconfirming signal, set before results;
- fallback if access fails or results remain ambiguous;
- decision consequence (continue investigating, narrow, pause, or none ready).

An artifact walkthrough, request for a warm introduction, or review of one existing incident can be more useful than a large interview campaign. Do not invent access. With no useful feasible test, say what is blocked and pause rather than prescribe busywork. Save in VALIDATION PLAN and summarize the current next action in OPEN QUESTIONS. Draft outreach only; do not send or publish without instruction.
